CFS Model Portfolio Weights

Last rebalance: 14 Aug 2026 Last updated: 23 Oct 2026 Owner: Anthony Nguyen Source: CFS

Model target weights. The weights on this page are the model target weights for each CFS portfolio. Client account holdings move away from these weights between rebalances as markets move.

Auto-rebalance schedule. The CFS portfolios are auto-rebalanced quarterly, on the 14th of February, May, August and November. A scheduled auto-rebalance is skipped if the Portfolio Manager has made a change to the portfolio since the previous rebalance. For example, if the Portfolio Manager implements a trade on 1 October 2026, the 14 November 2026 auto-rebalance is skipped and the next auto-rebalance is 14 February 2027.
