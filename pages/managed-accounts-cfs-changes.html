CFS Managed Accounts

Portfolio Changes Commentary

Last updated: 24 Aug 2026 Owner: Anthony Nguyen Source: CFS, Vantage Investment Committee

Rebalance

14 August 2026 Rebalance

CFS Model Portfolios, Active. Approved by the Vantage Investment Committee.

The August rebalance implements changes across Australian equities, global equities, property and infrastructure, and fixed income. The changes reduce portfolio risk and bring the CFS models into closer alignment with the Vantage HUB24 managed portfolios. Strategic asset allocation is unchanged at the asset class level across all five strategies, so every change sits within an existing sleeve and target weights are unaffected.

Australian Equities

Within the active portion of the sleeve, Vinva increases from 16.7% to 25.0%, Airlie moves from 16.7% to 15.0%, and First Sentier reduces from 16.7% to 10.0%. The index allocation holds at 50.0%. This continues the direction set in the Australian equities review completed in late February and early March, when DNR was removed on concerns around process, investment philosophy and risk management, and Vinva was introduced on the strength of its quantitative approach and risk controls. Airlie and First Sentier have both added value relative to benchmark since that review, and the recent rally in technology stocks was supportive for First Sentier's technology and software exposures, which made this an appropriate point to lock in gains and continue moving toward the structure agreed earlier in the year.

Global Equities

T. Rowe Price reduces from 34.7% of the sleeve to 12.5%, with Vinva Global Alpha introduced at 20.0% and Yarra Global Small Companies at 7.5%. The two index holdings each move from 32.6% to 30.0%. The change reduces the concentration currently sitting with a single active manager and introduces diversification through quantitative strategies already in use within the Vantage HUB24 portfolios. Yarra Global Small Companies was submitted to the investment committee on the internal Vantage checklist, while Vinva Global Alpha had already been approved.

Property and Infrastructure

The sleeve moves from an even split to 60% global listed infrastructure and 40% global property, consistent with the HUB24 portfolios. The preference for global infrastructure reflects our view that the asset class is better positioned to benefit from growing demand for electricity and power networks needed to support increasing data centre capacity and broader digitalisation.

Fixed Income

Bentham and PIMCO each reduce from 30.0% of the sleeve to 20.0%, while Perpetual and Schroder each increase from 20.0% to 30.0%. The CFS portfolio carried approximately double the interest rate duration of the Vantage fixed income portfolio on HUB24, at around four years against two years, and the change aligns the two. Perpetual and Schroder both hold between zero and one year of interest rate duration, so the reallocation lowers sensitivity to rate movements. While there is a reasonable argument that the rate hiking cycle is near its end, the economic outlook remains uncertain and reducing duration is the prudent position.
